How we shop-proof this store

Built with global marketplace habits in mind.

Shoppers arrive from apps like Jumia, Alibaba, and Temu, and they expect certain things: clear pricing, visible trust signals, easy comparison, and fast checkout. We borrowed the patterns that work — and left out the noise — to keep this a single, branded storefront rather than an open marketplace.

Trust signals, kept simple

Star ratings and reviews live on every service page, so buyers can judge quality before booking — the same at-a-glance trust cue used across large marketplaces.

Transparent, unit-based pricing

Every price shows its unit ("per item", "per session", "per event") up front — no surprise fees at checkout, a common complaint with cross-border marketplace orders.

Category-first browsing

A filterable sidebar and search bar mirror how shoppers narrow down catalogs of thousands of SKUs, scaled down to our own service range.

One-tap reorder

Signed-in customers can revisit past orders and bookings from their dashboard, making repeat print runs or annual event bookings quick to place again.

A note on scope

This storefront represents {{ company_name }} only — it isn't a multi-vendor marketplace. If you'd like your products listed on third-party platforms like Jumia, Alibaba, or Temu as an additional sales channel, that's a separate seller registration on each platform; we can help prepare product listings and photography for those channels on request.
